Learning goals & exam practice

Grade 1 builds number sense, phonics, reading, and sentence-writing foundations. Grade 3 adds multiplication, division, fractions, measurement, reading evidence, and written explanations.

Original practice informed by Massachusetts standards. MCAS starts in grade 3. This is supplemental practice, not an official test, a complete curriculum, or a guarantee of readiness for every exam. Memory is a fun attention break, not an MCAS skill assessment. Writing should be reviewed with a grown-up.
